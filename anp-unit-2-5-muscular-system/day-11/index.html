Focused notes

Muscles work in teams

  • The agonist (prime mover) is the main muscle that makes a movement.
  • The antagonist does the opposite movement, like the triceps during a biceps curl.
  • Synergists help the prime mover or hold the joint steady.

Notebook p. 65 · Focused Notes: Muscles work in teams
Working in groups

Synergists and antagonists

Muscles only pull — they cannot push. So they work in groups: synergists assist the main mover, and antagonists do the opposite action and relax to let it work.

Unit 2.5 Muscular System · Day 11 · Mon, Nov 2slide 6 of 32
Notebook p. 65 · Focused Notes: Muscles work in teams
A classic pair

Biceps and triceps

In a biceps curl, the biceps is the prime mover and shortens to bend the elbow. The triceps is the antagonist: it does the opposite job, straightening the elbow, and relaxes during the curl.

Head, neck, and trunk

  • Head: frontalis raises eyebrows, temporalis and masseter close the jaw.
  • Neck: sternocleidomastoid turns and bends the head.
  • Trunk: pectoralis major, rectus abdominis, external oblique, latissimus dorsi, and trapezius.

Notebook p. 66 · Focused Notes: Arms and legs
Focused notes

Arms and legs

  • Arm: deltoid lifts the arm, biceps brachii bends the elbow, triceps brachii straightens it.
  • Leg: gluteus maximus extends the hip, quadriceps straighten the knee, hamstrings bend it.
  • Lower leg: gastrocnemius points the toes, tibialis anterior lifts them.
