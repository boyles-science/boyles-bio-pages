Grip fatigue lab

Notebook pages 49 and 50 · Lab · 20 minutes · Groups. No supplies needed: you need your Chromebook and your notebook.

Grip Fatigue Lab. With a partner, squeeze for 60 seconds while they count every 10 seconds. Do 3 trials, then graph it.

Steps

  1. Squeeze your fist as many times as possible for 60 seconds. Your partner counts every 10 seconds. (Close your hand into a tight fist, then open it all the way. That is one squeeze.)
  2. Rest 1 minute, then repeat for 3 trials.
  3. Graph time (x-axis) versus repetitions (y-axis).
  4. Identify which energy system was working at the start and at the end.
How to use this page. The counter picks the trial and presses Start. After a short countdown, the counter presses the big button (or the space bar) once for each squeeze. The page records every 10 seconds for you. Then copy your numbers into the table in your notebook.

Working alone? Press the button yourself as fast as you can with one finger. That is not a grip, but your hand will still get tired.

Answer in your notebook.
  1. Which energy system was working at the start of the 60 seconds? Which was working at the end?
  2. Why did your repetitions drop over the 60 seconds?
  3. Compare trial 1 and trial 3. What changed, and why?
