Lab · Station 1 - LT1

Muscle types, jobs, fiber parts, and levels of organization

Prove It · no notes

1. Name the three types of muscle tissue and one location for each.

2. Which muscle type is striated and involuntary?

3. List the five jobs of the muscular system.

4. Which fiber part stores calcium? Which part makes ATP?

5. Put in order, smallest to largest: fascicle, myofibril, fiber, myofilament, whole muscle.

6. Which connective tissue wraps each fascicle?

Lab · Station 2 - LT2

Sliding filament theory, calcium and ATP, the NMJ, and contraction types

Prove It · no notes

1. What gets shorter when a muscle contracts: actin, myosin, or the sarcomere?

2. Name the four steps of the cross-bridge cycle in order.

3. What does calcium do? What does ATP do?

4. What chemical crosses the synaptic cleft at the NMJ?

5. How does the body make a stronger contraction?

6. Name the contraction type: holding a plank, lifting in a curl, lowering slowly.

Lab · Station 3 - LT3

ATP systems, fatigue, twitch and tetanus, and fiber types

Prove It · no notes
