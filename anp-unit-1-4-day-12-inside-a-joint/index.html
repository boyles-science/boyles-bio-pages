Inside a joint

No supplies needed. You need your notebook, a pencil, and this page.

Step 1 · Label the parts of a synovial joint

A synovial joint is a joint with a space inside it that holds fluid. Your knee is one. Your shoulder is another.

Do this in your notebook. Draw the joint with a pencil. Tap each part below. Write its name next to your drawing. Then write what it does in your own words.

Tap a part

Tap a button or a part of the picture. You will see its name and its job.

Step 2 · Test what each part does

Do this. Move the bend slider. Then switch off one part at a time. Watch the meters. In your notebook, write what changed for each part you switch off.

Step 3 · Compare a door hinge to a real joint

Do this. Go back to Step 2. Test the Door hinge. Then test the Real joint. Keep bending. Watch the wear meter. Then read the table.

A door hinge

  • Two flat parts and a pin.
  • Needs oil added from outside.
  • Wears out. It cannot repair itself.

A real joint

  • A cartilage cap on each bone end.
  • Fluid made inside the joint.
  • Capsule, ligaments, bursae, and menisci.
  • Living tissue that can respond.
Do this in your notebook. Make two columns: door hinge and real joint. Write two differences you saw in the test. Write one thing a door hinge cannot do.

Why injuries matter. A ligament connects bone to bone. A tendon connects muscle to bone. Cartilage has few blood vessels, so damage may heal slowly. Menisci in the knee absorb shock and improve the fit, and they can tear.
