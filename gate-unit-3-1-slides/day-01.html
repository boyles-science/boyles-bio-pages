Notes

Radioactive decay

Listen first, then copy into your notebook.

Write this:
  • Radioactive atoms are unstable. They break down (decay) into stable atoms.
  • A half-life is the time for half of the radioactive atoms in a sample to decay.
  • Radiometric dating uses the ratio of parent atoms to daughter atoms to find a rock's age.

Earth's age (taught only)

How old is Earth?

Try first: write your answer before we check.
  • Meteorites formed with the solar system. Radiometric dating gives them about 4.56 billion years.
  • Tiny zircon crystals from Jack Hills, Australia, are about 4.4 billion years old.
  • So Earth is about 4.5 billion years old. Very little of its first crust survives.
