Notes

Seafloor spreading

Listen first, then copy into your notebook.

Write this:
  • At a mid-ocean ridge, magma rises, cools, and forms new ocean crust.
  • The new crust moves away on both sides, so crust gets older with distance from the ridge.

Notes

Magnetic stripes

Listen first, then copy into your notebook.

Write this:
  • Cooling rock records the direction of Earth's magnetic field, which flips over time.
  • The stripes match in mirror image on both sides of a ridge: evidence that both sides formed together and moved apart.
