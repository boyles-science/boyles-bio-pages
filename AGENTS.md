# AGENTS.md — Michelle's student pages

**EVERYTHING IN THIS FOLDER IS PUBLIC TO THE WHOLE INTERNET.**
No answer keys, no teacher notes, no student data, no tokens, ever. Tools here never contain correct answers.

## Purpose
Student-facing pages for Michelle Boyles's biology classes, Highland High School (KHSD).
Published with GitHub Pages. Her curriculum source and all teacher material live in the private
`boyles-teaching` folder; its AGENTS.md rules apply here too.

## Rules
- One page per folder, a single `index.html` with no outside dependencies.
- Works at phone width (students open links on phones) and on school Chromebooks.
- Student text follows the writing rules: short sentences, second person, terms defined, no idioms.
- Never invent facts; every science fact must match the curriculum source.
- Save point after each finished change.

## Sub plans
- When Michelle asks for a sub plan, use her saved 3-page format. Never write sub plans in this repo; they stay in Google Drive.
- The format, the generator and her standing information are in the Drive file "Sub plan format (for Claude) - keep this file.txt". Read it first.
