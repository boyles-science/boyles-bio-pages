Terms

New words

  • Chromosome: a long strand of DNA carrying many genes.
  • Chromosome pair: two matching chromosomes, one from each of your parents.
  • Meiosis: cell division that makes sex cells with half the chromosomes.
  • Sex cell: a sperm or egg; also called a gamete.

Model

Build the model

Use two colors: one color for chromosomes from Grandma, one for Grandpa.

  • Make 2 pairs (one long, one short).
  • Write an allele on each: Pair 1 has A and a. Pair 2 has B and b.
  • A sex cell gets one chromosome from each pair.

Crossing over

Swap pieces

Before pairs separate, matching chromosomes can swap matching pieces. This is crossing over.

Add a second gene to Pair 1: C on Grandma's, c on Grandpa's. Swap the ends that carry C and c.

Write this: What new allele mixes do you get on one chromosome?
