Term

Mutagen

Mutagen: something in the environment that can damage DNA and cause mutations. Examples: UV light, X-rays, some chemicals in tobacco smoke.

Control: a group that does not get the factor being tested.

Real study

X-rays and fruit flies

In 1927, Hermann Muller exposed fruit flies to X-rays. Offspring of exposed flies showed many more new inherited traits than offspring of flies that were not exposed.

Write this: Which source of variation does this show? Why did the changes reach the offspring?
