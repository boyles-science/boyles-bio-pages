Notes

Copying DNA

Listen first, then copy into your notebook.

Write this:
  • Before a cell divides, it copies its DNA using the pairing rule: A with T, C with G.
  • Cells proofread and fix most mistakes. A few slip through: these are copying errors.
  • A copying error that is not fixed is a mutation: a change in the base order.

Notebook: Notes
Notes

Which mutations are inherited?

Listen first, then copy into your notebook.

Write this:
  • Body cells are all cells except sperm and eggs. Their mutations are not passed on.
  • Only mutations in sex cells, or the cells that make them, can reach a child.
  • Many mutations change nothing. Some change a protein (Unit 2.1).

Terms

New words

  • Mutation: a change in the base order of DNA.
  • Copying error: a wrong base added when DNA is copied, and not fixed.
  • Body cell: any cell that is not a sperm or egg.
