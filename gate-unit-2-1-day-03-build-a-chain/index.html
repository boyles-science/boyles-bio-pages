Read first

Terms

  • A gene is a section of DNA. DNA has two strands.
  • RNA is a copy of one strand. It has U where DNA has T.
  • A codon is a group of three bases in the RNA. Each codon stands for one amino acid.
  • A protein is made of a chain of amino acids.

Pairing rule for copying the bottom strand into RNA:
T → A   A → U   C → G   G → C
