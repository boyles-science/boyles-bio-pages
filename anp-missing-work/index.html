Anatomy: Find Your Missing Work

Use your own Chromebook. This takes about two minutes. The list in Canvas is only yours.

1

Open your grades

Go to Canvas. Click Grades in the left menu. Then pick Anatomy & Physiology if it asks.
Open my Anatomy grades →

2

Look for Missing

Missing means you have not turned it in yet. Scroll the whole list. Write each missing name in your notebook.

3

Also check for zeros or no score

A dash (–) means no score yet. Some items may need you to fix or finish them. If you are not sure, ask your teacher.

4

Do the oldest one first

Click the name. Read the directions. Finish it in Canvas, or scan your notebook pages to one PDF and upload it. Press Submit.

5

Check that it worked

Go back to Grades. The word Missing should be gone. It can take a few minutes. If it stays, tell your teacher.

Tip: Turn in your work yourself. Do not wait for someone to remind you. Ask for help if you are stuck.
