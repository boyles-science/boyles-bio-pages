Evidence cards

Four kinds of evidence

  1. Seafloor ages: youngest at ridges, oldest near trenches.
  2. GPS: North America and Europe move apart about 2.5 cm per year.
  3. Hawaiian islands: Kaua'i is about 5 million years old; the island of Hawai'i is under 1 million years old and still volcanic.
  4. Matching fossils and rocks: fossils of Mesosaurus, a freshwater reptile, are found in both South America and Africa.
