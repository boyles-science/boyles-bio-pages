Reliable sources

Where to look

Good sources
  • National Institutes of Health (NIH)
  • MedlinePlus
  • Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
  • A medical center or university hospital
Not enough on their own
  • A blog or a forum post
  • A video with no named organization
  • A page that sells a product

Name the organization behind every source. You need at least 3.
