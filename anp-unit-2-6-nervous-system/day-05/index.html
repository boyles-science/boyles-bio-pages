Essential question

How does a signal get from one neuron to the next?

Neurons do not touch. A synapse is the tiny gap where one cell passes a signal to another.

Notebook · Notes box 1
The synapse

Where cells meet

  • The presynaptic cell sends the signal. Its axon terminal holds vesicles, small sacs of neurotransmitter.
  • The synaptic cleft is the gap.
  • The postsynaptic cell receives the signal. It has receptors that neurotransmitters fit into.
  • The neuromuscular junction is a synapse.

General example. A synapse is like the handoff in a relay race.

Specific example. At the neuromuscular junction, a motor neuron hands the signal to a muscle fiber.

Notebook · Notes box 3
Five steps

How the signal crosses

1
An action potential reaches the axon terminal.
2
Calcium ions (Ca²⁺) enter the terminal.
3
Vesicles release neurotransmitter into the cleft.
4
Neurotransmitter binds to receptors on the receiving cell.
5
The neurotransmitter is cleared away.

General example. It is like ringing a doorbell. The press becomes a chime, and then someone answers the door.

Specific example. When you decide to move a finger, ACh crosses the gap and your muscle fiber contracts.

Notebook · Back page: Stations
Partner stations
Paper-ball model

Model a synapse

1
Trial 1. One person is the sending cell with 5 paper balls (neurotransmitter). Another holds a cup (the receptors) 50 cm away. Toss all 5 balls. Count how many land in the cup.
2
Trial 2: blocked receptors. Cover half of the cup opening with a notebook. Toss 5 balls again. Count the balls that land in the cup.
3
Trial 3: more neurotransmitter. Toss 10 balls with the cup open. Count again.

Use soft paper balls and toss gently. About 3 minutes for each trial.

Summarize

How does a signal get from one neuron to the next?

A synapse is the gap where one cell passes a signal to another. An action potential opens calcium channels. Vesicles release neurotransmitter into the cleft. Neurotransmitter binds receptors and excites or inhibits the next cell. Enzymes and pumps then clear it away.
