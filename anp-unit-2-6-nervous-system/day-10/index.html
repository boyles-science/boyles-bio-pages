Injury and infection

Concussion and meningitis

  • A concussion happens when the brain moves inside the skull after a blow. It can cause headache, confusion, and trouble with balance. Rest and medical care help.
  • Meningitis is inflammation of the meninges. It can come from viruses or bacteria. Symptoms include fever, headache, and a stiff neck. Bacterial meningitis is an emergency.

General example. A concussion is like a computer that freezes. The hardware is fine, but it works poorly for a while.

Specific example. A soccer player is dazed after a header and has a headache.

Diseases of signaling

Multiple sclerosis and cerebral palsy

  • In multiple sclerosis (MS), the immune system attacks myelin in the CNS.
  • Without myelin, signals slow or stop.
  • Cerebral palsy is a group of movement disorders. It is caused by brain damage or abnormal brain development before, during, or soon after birth.

Addiction

The reward pathway

  • Dopamine is part of the brain's reward pathway.
  • Many drugs raise dopamine. With repeated use, the brain adjusts. This can cause tolerance and dependence.
  • Addiction is a medical condition. People can recover with help.
  • If you or someone you know needs help, talk to a school counselor, a nurse, or a trusted adult.

General example. Dopamine is like a signal that says "do that again."

Specific example. Repeated use of a drug changes how the reward pathway responds.

Summarize

What happens when the nervous system is damaged or changed?

Disorders and drugs change nervous system signaling. A concussion injures the brain. Meningitis inflames the meninges. MS damages myelin, and cerebral palsy affects movement. Drugs change synapses by blocking receptors or reuptake. The nervous system connects to every other system.
