Anatomy · Unit 1.4 · Day 8Fuller picture

Structure fits the job

Reach. A mobile shoulder girdle and a long humerus let the arm swing through a large range.
Rotate. Radius and ulna are two bones so the radius can cross over the ulna.
Grip. Many small hand bones (8 carpals, 5 metacarpals, 14 phalanges) create precision and fine motor control.
Every hand has 27 bones: 8 + 5 + 14. Keep the count light; the focus is the arrangement.
